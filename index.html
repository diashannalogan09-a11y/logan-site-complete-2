Our Story

Logan Pathway to Hope & Healing was created from a deep belief that youth and families deserve more than temporary solutions.

They deserve a place where healing is intentional, support is consistent, and every person is treated with dignity.

Our work is rooted in compassion, structure, and hope. We understand that many youth and families come to services carrying trauma, stress, broken trust, and uncertainty.

Whether through outpatient therapy, group counseling, assessments, or future program development, Logan Pathway exists to help people move from surviving to healing, and from healing to purpose.
