OUR STORY

Care with purpose.
Healing with heart.

Logan Pathway to Hope & Healing was created to make care feel personal, intentional, and deeply connected to real change.

We believe healing should feel human.

Logan Pathway was created from a deep belief that individuals and families deserve more than temporary solutions.

Too often, people enter services carrying trauma, stress, broken trust, uncertainty, and emotional heaviness without receiving the type of consistent support that helps them truly feel seen and understood.

We built Logan Pathway to be different. Our work is grounded in compassion, structure, emotional wellness, and the belief that healing begins when people feel safe enough to be honest and supported enough to grow.

Through outpatient therapy, group counseling, clinical assessments, and future program development, we are creating a pathway from survival to healing and from healing to purpose.

LEADERSHIP

Two leaders. One shared mission.

Logan Pathway is led with clinical vision, community impact, and a commitment to care that reaches people in meaningful ways.

Founder & Visionary

Diashanna Logan

Diashanna Logan is the Founder and Visionary behind Logan Pathway to Hope & Healing. As a therapist, her work is grounded in trauma-informed care, emotional wellness, crisis intervention, and helping individuals build tools for real growth.

She supports individuals navigating anxiety, depression, trauma, family conflict, emotional regulation, life transitions, and stress. Her approach is structured, compassionate, honest, and empowering.

Diashanna believes healing work should create safety, restore confidence, and help people reconnect with purpose.

Co-Founder & Chief Impact Officer

Amber Jones-Logan

Amber Jones-Logan serves as Co-Founder and Chief Impact Officer, helping shape the heart, direction, and community impact of Logan Pathway to Hope & Healing.

Her leadership strengthens the organization’s focus on connection, access, advocacy, partnerships, and meaningful outcomes.

Amber helps ensure the mission reaches the people who need it most and that every service reflects the purpose behind Logan Pathway.
